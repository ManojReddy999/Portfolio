Real MuJoCo physics & LiDAR · EKF + scan-matching SLAM · stand-in planner (trained policy next)

The only light is what its LiDAR can see right now — anything behind an obstacle stays dark. The glowing map on the floor is what it has seen before and remembers.

Physics: MuJoCo 3.12 compiled to WebAssembly, loading the exact scene my navigation policy trains in — verified bit-identical to the Python simulator.
Sensing: a 360-ray, 4 m LiDAR cast by MuJoCo from where the MuSHR car mounts its YDLidar.
Driver: a classical stand-in — it samples steering arcs, checks the car's real footprint against every LiDAR return, and takes the longest clear path. My trained depth-camera policy replaces it next.
SLAM: the car doesn't know where it is. It dead-reckons from wheel encoders and a gyro in an EKF, tracks against a map of its recent route, and learns its own gyro bias and wheel scale as it goes. When it comes back somewhere it mapped long ago, it recognises the place, and loop closure corrects the whole route and map. The sensor errors are synthetic.
