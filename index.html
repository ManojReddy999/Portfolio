UC San Diego · Existential Robotics Lab

Learning to drive from depth alone

PPO policies that drive the same car through cluttered arenas from a forward depth camera. I built the environment at UCSD, then in 2026 rebuilt the evaluation and re-ran the work properly.

  • Judged on 100–300 held-out scenes, with pass/fail rules written before each run
  • Found and fixed simulator bugs that had shaped earlier results
  • Some earlier findings held up and some didn’t; the write-up says which

About

Mechanical engineer turned roboticist

Open to robotics, RL and autonomy roles · based in India · remote preferred

I studied mechanical engineering at IIT Bombay, then did a master’s in robotics at UC San Diego. There I was a graduate student researcher in Prof. Nikolay Atanasov’s Existential Robotics Laboratory, working on reinforcement learning for autonomous navigation.

Back in India, as AI Tech Lead I built two systems that are now in production: an HR and payroll platform a company’s staff use, and a chatbot that answers field teams’ questions from their database. Alongside that, I have rebuilt my navigation research with a much stricter evaluation.

I am looking for work where I own learning or estimation systems end to end: simulation, training, evaluation, and getting them in front of people.
