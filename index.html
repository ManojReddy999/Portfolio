UC San Diego · Existential Robotics Lab

Learning to drive from depth alone

Deep RL policies (PPO, SAC) that drive an Ackermann-steered car through clutter from onboard depth sensing, trained in a MuJoCo simulator I built and deployed on a real F1TENTH car.

  • Graph network over the CNN: +21% speed, +85% distance covered, −17% collisions
  • Cross-modal transformer over the graph network: +20% mean velocity, +135% coverage, −27% collisions
  • Ran onboard the car’s Jetson TX2 via ONNX and TensorRT
  • MuJoCo
  • dm_control
  • PPO / SAC
  • PyTorch
  • TensorRT
